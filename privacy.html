SizeRight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

SizeRight does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • The numbers you type (balance, prices, fees) are saved only on your own device so the app remembers them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • The app works fully offline and makes no network requests except to update its own files.

SizeRight is a calculator, not financial advice.
